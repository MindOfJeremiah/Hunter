🌸while you’re there
  • Ballona Wetlands — bike path along the water, calm
  • Manhattan Beach Pier — 10min south, cleaner vibe if you want to walk
🌬️step away spots
  • Walk north past lot 3 — beach thins out fast, no lifeguards, more space
  • Grass strip between the lots and PCH — low foot traffic, good wind cover from the dunes
  • South end near the jetty — far from the families, good view
